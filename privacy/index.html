Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Overview

BlockSpark is an Android application for creating Minecraft Bedrock add-ons. We designed the app to keep as much information as possible on your device and to disclose when information must leave the device to provide a requested feature.

Information stored on your device

BlockSpark stores app preferences and creation history in private app storage. Generated .mcaddon files you export are saved to Downloads/BlockSpark and remain there until you delete them.

AI generation

When you request AI generation, the description you enter and any optional reference image you select may be transmitted to the AI service used by the app so the requested add-on can be generated. BlockSpark does not need those materials for unrelated purposes.

BlockSpark is preparing a Continue with ChatGPT connection for eligible users. The exact production authentication and plan-usage flow will be documented here before public release.

Safety reports

If you voluntarily report generated content as unsafe, BlockSpark may send the report category, app/model metadata, timestamps, and a one-way hash that identifies the generated specification. Reports are designed not to include your prompt, reference image, generated file contents, or authentication credentials.

Analytics and advertising

The current BlockSpark app does not include advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral advertising.

Data deletion

You can clear BlockSpark's private local data from inside the app or through Android system settings. Exported .mcaddon files can be deleted through your file manager.

Children

BlockSpark is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise account-based AI service use in accordance with the applicable service's terms.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when material data practices change.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the BlockSpark support page.

BlockSpark is an independent product. It is not an official Minecraft product and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
