FAQ

The practical stuff.

Is BlockSpark an official Minecraft app?+

No. BlockSpark is an independent creation tool and is not approved by, sponsored by, or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

What does the $5 include?+

The planned Play Store price is a one-time $5 purchase for the Android app. AI service access is separate from the app purchase.

Can I use my ChatGPT subscription?+

That is the launch target. Continue with ChatGPT support is being prepared so eligible users can connect without pasting an API key. Commercial authorization from OpenAI is currently pending.

Does it work with Realms?+

Yes. Activate the generated packs on a local Bedrock world first, then upload or replace the Realm world with that configured world.
