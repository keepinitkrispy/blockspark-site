SUPPORT & INSTALLATION

Get your BlockSpark add-on into Bedrock.

BlockSpark exports a .mcaddon file containing the generated Bedrock packs. The exact Minecraft menu wording can move between Bedrock updates, but the workflow stays the same.

01
⇩

Generate and export

Finish the BlockSpark creation flow. The exported file is saved to Downloads/BlockSpark on Android.

02
▦

Open with Minecraft

Tap Open with Minecraft after generation, or open the .mcaddon from Downloads and choose Minecraft. Wait for import confirmation.

03
✓

Activate the packs

Create or edit a world. In Behavior Packs and Resource Packs, activate the imported BlockSpark pack or packs before entering the world.

04
↑

Move it to Realms

Make sure the add-on works locally first. Then upload or replace your Realm world with that configured world so its active pack configuration travels with it.

If something does not behave correctly

Import success only proves Minecraft accepted the pack. Use BlockSpark's Not quite right? Fix it flow and describe exactly what happened in-game.
